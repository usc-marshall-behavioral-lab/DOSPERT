The finding — from your own data.
 Research using this scale found that within-person variation across domains is roughly 7 times larger than between-person variation.1 You are not a uniformly risky or cautious person — you are a contextual one. For autonomous vehicle trust, this means operator selection based on general risk tolerance is a weak design strategy. The situation, the system design, and the information environment do most of the work. Trust calibration is a system design problem — not a personnel problem.
  1. Blais, A. R., & Weber, E. U. (2006). A domain-specific risk-taking (DOSPERT) scale for adult populations. Judgment and Decision Making, 1(1), 33–47.
